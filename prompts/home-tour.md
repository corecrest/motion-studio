# Creative brief: architectural home tour

Only use supplied home photographs as visual truth. Do not invent rooms or viewpoints unsupported by pictures.
Code animation can create editorial pans, parallax over photos, transitions, title cards and video assembly.
Real camera movement through unobserved geometry requires separate 3D reconstruction or image-to-video tools and verification.
Use licensed or supplied audio. Produce a storyboard and sample frames before rendering.
