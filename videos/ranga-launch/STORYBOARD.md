# Your money. Your move.

A 25-second portrait teaser for Ranga. Cream and forest green frame a dark, flat Android interface. An editorial rule acts as a recurring transition and leads into the wordmark reveal. The campaign reads in silence.

| Time | Picture and motion | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00–04 | A green rule draws in. Two lines rise in sequence with a quiet hold. | Your money. Your move. |
| 04–08 | Reveal the phone against muted sage. The Home overview and two account rows remain in view. | Everything in view. |
| 08–14 | Keep the phone anchored. Enter 5,000, choose a source and saved beneficiary, review, then show the USSD handoff. | Make your move. |
| 14–19 | Scroll an Activities timeline and open an earlier recorded expense. | Keep track. |
| 19–23 | A green panel sweeps up. The wordmark resolves against cream. | ranga |
| 23–25 | Hold the brand lockup and add the end line. | Pay. Track. Stay in control. |

## Asset and product direction

Use the user's original Ranga wordmark splash as the artwork source. Rebuild the Home, Pay and Activities views as editable HTML based on the Ranga references. All figures, beneficiary names and receipt data are fictional. The handoff ends at the payment channel, with no success state for the initiated payment.

## Sound direction

Original synthesized bed with cues at 0.45 and 1.05 s for the two headline beats, 4 and 14 s for device transitions, 8.70–9.30 s for keypad ticks, 13.18 s for the USSD handoff, and 20.1 s for the wordmark. Keep all cues short and understated.
